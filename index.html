🎯 How to get useful feedback

  • Place the phone securely at about waist-to-chest height, not in your hand.
  • Use bright, even lighting. The framing requirement changes with your selected drill.
  • Start with a relaxed stance and guard. Do not throw hard punches near your phone.
  • Move slowly at first. The detector may lose hands or ankles when they move quickly or leave the frame.

Safety first

Train in clear space. Do not use this prototype for sparring, impact timing, injury assessment, or as proof that your technique is correct. Stop if you feel pain, dizzy, or unwell.

Simple habits that help

  • Eat at reasonably regular times. Add a snack if large meals are difficult.
  • Use everyday foods such as roti, rice, poha, potatoes, dal, chole, rajma, seasonal vegetables, fruit, milk or suitable alternatives.
  • Include a protein source across the day, choosing foods that fit your budget and preference.
  • Drink water regularly and keep sleep as consistent as you can.

This is general guidance, not a medical diet. If your weight changes unexpectedly, you have ongoing symptoms, or you need a condition-specific plan, speak with a qualified healthcare professional. If you are under 18, avoid using adult weight-loss targets.
